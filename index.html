What is Lokai?

A partner for the work, not another tab to manage.

Most AI products begin and end with a chat window. Lokai is the operating layer underneath the conversation. It can find the right capability, bring in grounded context, run local or optional external models, use approved tools, preserve project state, and leave a receipt of what happened.

How it works

One request. A governed path to an outcome.

The architecture stays out of your way, but it remains visible enough to earn trust. Lokai separates deterministic control from model generation so authority does not depend on a model remembering the rules.

  1. 1

    Understand and route

    Your goal enters a deterministic control plane.

    Lokai selects relevant context, available capabilities, hardware, policy, and authority before execution begins.

  2. 2

    Compose and work

    The smallest useful set of intelligence goes to work.

    A local model, a mixture of models, tools, skills, or optional cloud capacity can be composed around the task.

  3. 3

    Verify and remember

    Results return with state, evidence, and operator control.

    Lokai checks the outcome, records the useful trace, asks when authority is needed, and carries the project forward.

FAQ

Direct answers before launch.

Does my data have to leave my hardware?

No. Lokai is local-first, and private user state stays local by default. External services or cloud models are optional choices with their own explicit boundaries.

Does Lokai include one required model?

No. Models are optional packs selected for the task, hardware, and upstream license. Lokai can operate one model, compose multiple models, or route work to another trusted worker.

Can I use a cloud model?

Yes, when you choose to connect one. Local-first is an ownership posture, not a ban on useful external capacity.

Will the public stack cost money to unlock capabilities?

No. The intended public/freeware stack is fully capable and non-degraded. Optional third-party services, models, and hardware can still carry their own costs and licenses.

Is Lokai open source?

The intended launch posture is freeware with protected compiled engine and routing implementations, not a fully open-source core. Public interfaces, tooling, docs, and approved components remain inspectable; protected source and private keys do not ship.

Do enterprise customers receive a stronger hidden version?

No. They receive a custom deployment of the same full system: commercial scope, private integrations, private data mapping, private packs, white-label work, validation, support, and service terms tailored to their environment.

Can I keep using an agent harness I already know?

That is the intended architecture. Lokai is being built to sit beneath compatible harnesses as the governance, memory, runtime, and auditability layer rather than forcing one interface on every user.
